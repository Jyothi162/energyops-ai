
EnergyOps AI - Billing FAQ

Customers may contact billing support for questions about invoices, payment status, payment dates, and billing corrections.

If a customer believes an invoice is incorrect, the billing team should review the invoice and account information.

Customers should provide their customer ID and invoice reference when contacting billing support.

The AI assistant must not claim that a payment has been received or an invoice has been corrected unless this information has been verified through an authorized billing system.

Billing disputes that cannot be resolved automatically should be referred to a human support representative.

Billing Questions and Invoice Information

Q: How can I understand the charges on my solar electricity bill?
A: Customers should review the itemized charges on their
electricity bill or solar energy invoice. For clarification
about billing amounts, fees, or charges, they should contact
the billing support team with their customer ID and invoice
reference.

Q: Where can I find information about my solar energy invoice?
A: Customers should refer to their invoice or contact the
billing support team for invoice information. They should
provide their customer ID and invoice reference so that
the billing team can review the relevant account information.

Q: What should I do if my solar energy invoice is incorrect?
A: Contact billing support and request a review of the
invoice and account information. The AI assistant must not
claim that an invoice has been corrected without verification
from an authorized billing system.
